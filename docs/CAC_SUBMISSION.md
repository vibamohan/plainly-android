# Competition submission checklist

- [ ] Verify official 2026 Congressional App Challenge rules and deadline in the official submission portal.
- [ ] Run `./scripts/run-device-smoke-test.sh` on a physical Android device or emulator.
- [ ] Test a school notice, credential request, benign notice, PDF, image, camera capture, translation, and airplane mode after model setup.
- [ ] Record measured device OCR/analysis/translation latency and observed errors.
- [ ] Film a truthful 2–3 minute demo showing evidence and limitations.
- [ ] Review privacy, AI disclosure, licenses, and model downloads.
- [ ] Submit through the official portal.
