# AI assistance disclosure

AI assistance was used throughout development of Plainly. It generated the initial Android project and contributed to Kotlin analysis rules, OCR and document-import flows, the Compose interface, translation and speech features, local history and export, tests, evaluation scripts, and project documentation.

AI assistance also drafted the fictional sample letters and synthetic evaluation examples. These examples are not student-authored or real correspondence.

The project owner should review the code, wording, sources, and test results before submission, and describe their own contributions accurately. Device testing and review of the final submission materials remain the project owner's responsibility.
